Choose the route that creates the best risk-adjusted income and fastest credible time to market.

OptionChoose when
Reuse directlyOwnership, boundaries, releases, and support are already compatible.
Extract service/libraryReuse is material and a stable shared contract can be owned.
Connect via API/MCPIndependence, permissions, or different release cadences matter.
